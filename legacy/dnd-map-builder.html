About Lighting
In Player View, cells are pitch black until a token's vision reaches them and they're lit — by a torch/prop, or by a token carrying light. Tokens with darkvision see within their radius even unlit. Previously-seen cells stay dimly visible as memory once vision moves away, like a real party's recollection of a room.
